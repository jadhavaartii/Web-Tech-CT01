Theory – Introduction to Internet

The Internet is a worldwide network of interconnected computers and devices that allows people to communicate, access information, and share resources. It has become an important part of everyday life and is widely used for education, business, entertainment, communication, and online services.

**Email (Electronic Mail)** is an Internet-based service used to send and receive messages electronically. It can also be used to send documents, images, and other files as attachments.

**World Wide Web (WWW)** is a collection of interconnected web pages and websites available through the Internet. Users can access these pages using web browsers such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge. The WWW provides information through text, images, videos, and interactive content.

**Broadband** refers to a high-speed Internet connection that provides faster data transmission than traditional dial-up connections. It can be provided through fiber-optic cables, DSL, cable networks, or wireless technologies.

**FTP (File Transfer Protocol)** is a communication protocol used to transfer files between computers over a network. It allows users to upload files to a server and download files from it.

Thus, the Internet and its major services provide fast, convenient, and efficient ways to communicate, access information, and transfer data across the world.
